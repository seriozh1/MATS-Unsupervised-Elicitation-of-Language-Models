TRAIN AN AI ASSISTANT TO BE HELPFUL AND HONEST


This project is confidential. Don't share any details.


  • Chat with the AI to get help with any text-based task . Just treat the AI like a friend or an expert. If it goes off-topic or makes a mistake, you can just tell it directly what's wrong in your next message.
  • The AI will provide two possible responses, A and B. Choose the most helpful and honest response. This is the most important part, and it's how the AI will learn and improve. If the responses seem about the same, or you are unsure about which one to pick, click a smaller A or B button.
  • Be warned: the AI may fabricate information and website addresses. Fact check it if you can—lying isn't helpful!
